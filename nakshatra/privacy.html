Privacy Policy

What the app does with your information, in plain terms.

Last updated: 4 September 2026

The short version. Your birth details never leave your phone. All astrological calculations run on your device, and we operate no server that receives them. The only data leaving your phone goes to Google and our payment provider, for advertising, crash reporting and purchases.

This policy explains what Nakshatra (“the app”) does with your information. Nakshatra is published by HeloCode Labs and distributed on Google Play under the package name io.helocode.nakshatra.

01Birth details you enter

To produce a birth chart, nekath timings and compatibility results, the app asks for a date of birth, a time of birth and a birth place.

  • Stored only in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • Not transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • Never used for advertising or profiling — only to produce your readings.
  • Deleting the app deletes it. We cannot recover it, because we never had it.

If you save profiles for family members, the same applies to their details. Please only enter another person’s birth information if they are content for you to do so.

02What is sent off your device

The app uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy, linked below.

ServicePurposeWhat it may receive
Google AdMob Advertisements in the free version Advertising ID, approximate location from IP address, device and app information, ad interactions
Firebase Analytics Understanding which features are used Anonymous usage events, device model, language, country
Firebase Crashlytics Diagnosing crashes Crash reports, stack traces, device state at the time of a crash
RevenueCat Validating purchases and subscriptions An anonymous purchase identifier and receipt data
Google Play Billing Processing payments Handled entirely by Google. We never see or store card details.

None of these services receive your birth date, birth time or birth place.

03Advertising

The free version shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob, which may use your device’s Advertising ID to show more relevant ads.

  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads on your device.
  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom you will be asked for consent before personalised advertising is used, and can change that choice later in the app’s settings.
  • Purchasing “Remove Ads” or any Pro subscription stops advertisements entirely.

04Permissions

PermissionWhy
InternetLoading advertisements and validating purchases. Charts and nekath work fully offline.
NotificationsOptional daily horoscope and poya reminders. Declining restricts nothing else.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

05Children

Nakshatra is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will address it.

06Data retention and deletion

Because your birth details are stored only on your device, you control them entirely. To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Settings → Apps → Nakshatra → Storage. Data held by the third parties listed above is governed by their own retention policies, and each provides its own controls for deletion.

07Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data held about you, and to object to certain processing. Since we do not operate a server or hold your data ourselves, most such requests are best directed to the relevant third-party service. We will help where we can — contact us below.

08Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The “last updated” date at the top always reflects the current version, and material changes will be announced in the app.
