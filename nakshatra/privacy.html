Privacy Policy — Nakshatra

Last updated: 4 September 2026

This policy explains what Nakshatra (“the app”) does with your information. Nakshatra is published by HeloCode Labs. The app is distributed on Google Play under the package name io.helocode.nakshatra.

The short version. Your birth details never leave your phone. All astrological calculations run on your device, and we operate no server that receives them. The only data that leaves your phone goes to Google and our payment provider, for advertising, crash reporting and purchases.

1. Birth details you enter

To produce a birth chart, nekath timings and compatibility results, the app asks for a date of birth, a time of birth and a birth place.

  • This information is stored only in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • It is not transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • It is not used for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than producing your readings.
  • Deleting the app deletes it. We cannot recover it, because we never had it.

If you save profiles for family members, the same applies to their details. Please only enter another person’s birth information if they are content for you to do so.

2. What is sent off your device

The app uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy, linked below.

ServicePurposeWhat it may receive
Google AdMob Showing advertisements in the free version Advertising ID, approximate location derived from IP address, device and app information, ad interaction data
Firebase Analytics Understanding which features are used Anonymous usage events, device model, language, country
Firebase Crashlytics Diagnosing crashes Crash reports, stack traces, device state at the time of a crash
RevenueCat Validating purchases and subscriptions An anonymous purchase identifier and receipt data
Google Play Billing Processing payments Handled entirely by Google. We never see or store card details.

None of these services receive your birth date, birth time or birth place.

3. Advertising

The free version of the app shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob, which may use your device’s Advertising ID to show more relevant ads.

  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you will be asked for consent before personalised advertising is used, and you may change that choice later in the app’s settings.
  • Purchasing “Remove Ads” or any Pro subscription stops advertisements entirely.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

5. Children

Nakshatra is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will address it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because your birth details are stored only on your device, you control them entirely. To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Settings → Apps → Nakshatra → Storage. Data held by the third parties listed above is governed by their own retention policies; each provides its own controls for deletion.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data held about you, and to object to certain processing. Since we do not operate a server or hold your data ourselves, most such requests are best directed to the relevant third-party service. We will help where we can — contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The “last updated” date at the top will always reflect the current version. Material changes will be announced in the app.
